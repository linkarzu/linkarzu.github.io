Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how I, Christian Arzu, handle information when you
visit [linkarzu.com](https://linkarzu.com) or contact me about the site.

## Information collected

I do not require you to create an account to read this site. Information may be
collected in the following situations:

- **Site usage:** Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics may collect
  information about visits, such as pages viewed, approximate location, device
  and browser information, referral source, and interaction data. This helps me
  understand how the site is used and improve its content.
- **Technical data:** GitHub Pages, content delivery networks, and other service
  providers may process IP addresses, request details, and diagnostic or
  security logs when delivering the site.
- **Comments:** Blog comments are provided by Giscus and GitHub. If you choose
  to comment, GitHub processes your authentication and publishes the comment
  using your GitHub identity.
- **Messages:** If you contact me, I receive the information you choose to
  provide, such as your name, email address, and message.
- **Embedded and external content:** Pages may include YouTube videos, social
  media links, affiliate links, or other third-party content. Those services may
  receive technical and usage information when you load or interact with their
  content.

## Cookies and local storage

The site and its third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies
for analytics, security, preferences, and embedded content. The site also uses
browser local storage to remember when you dismiss the merchandise banner.

You can restrict cookies or clear cookies and local storage through your browser
settings. Doing so may affect embedded content or other site features.

## How information is used

Information is used to:

- Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the site.
- Understand which content is useful to visitors.
- Respond to questions, collaboration requests, and other messages.
- Detect abuse and protect the site and its visitors.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.

I do not sell your personal information. I do not use information collected by
this site to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant
effects on you.

## Third-party services

The site relies on services that process information under their own terms and
privacy policies, including:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for Google Tag
  Manager and Google Analytics.
- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
  for GitHub Pages and Giscus comments.
- [YouTube Privacy Guidelines](https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/user-settings/privacy/)
  for embedded videos.

Following a link to another website or service means that its privacy practices,
not this policy, apply to your activity there.

## Retention

I keep messages and other information only for as long as reasonably necessary
for the purposes described above, including maintaining business records,
resolving disputes, securing the site, and complying with legal obligations.
Analytics, hosting, comments, and embedded-content providers determine retention
for information they process under their own policies and settings.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal
information, such as requesting access, correction, or deletion, or objecting to
certain processing. You may also:

- Use browser privacy controls to limit cookies and local storage.
- Remove a comment through GitHub or contact me for assistance.
- Contact me to ask what information I hold about you or to make a privacy
  request.

I may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Some information
may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and recordkeeping
purposes.

## Children's privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through the site, contact me so I can review and delete it
when appropriate.

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the site, its services, or applicable requirements
change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest
revision.
