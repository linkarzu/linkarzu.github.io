## I added too much to my plate

Don't worry, this is already part of my menstrual cycle, the ups, and downs that
happen every now and then.

On a desperate attempt to try to gain more YouTube members, I duplicated the
amount of livestreams that members can participate in every month. Originally
for the $50 membership you could show up in the channel once a month.

But with the new changes, for the $50 you would get 2 livestreams a month.

My original and founding CEO Rumen, who I'm really grateful for, had already
been paying the $50 for a year. So I doubled his episodes from 12 to 24 for that
year he had already paid and we've been catching up on those episodes,
livestreaming once a week.

The experiment to get more paying members didn't work the way I thought it
would. I assumed that having a $25 membership would make at least 10 new people
join, so that making these livestreams would make sense to me, financially.

But it turns out I was wrong, 2 new members joined. My man `Techieman` who
joined for the $50 membership and my boy Cashmere for the $25 one. I'm not
saying I'm not grateful for their support, of course I am, as I really don't
really make money out of YouTube and their memberships significantly support the
channel. Even more than the joke of an amount I get from YouTube Ads revenue.

In summary, these changes added way more to my plate, without the financial
return I thought they would bring, and if I continue this way I know I'll just
burn out. Getting there.

So I'll move back to the original deal. The $50 membership gets 1 livestream a
month. And I'll include a new one, the $25 one gets 1 livestream every 2 months
in the Root Access podcast. No $100 or any other higher paying memberships (for
now)

[Watch the Linkarzu Root Access Podcast on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZWMav2s1MZR_E0s7OTicc7Th5QuWvUPx){:target="\_blank"}

I will keep a commitment of 1 livestream per month with Members. I don't want to
be tied to a schedule as my day job and life are already tight enough. I'll keep
doing my solo livestreams when I feel the desire to do so. Could be once every 2
months, could be once a week, once every couple of days, nobody knows, but
there's no pressure and schedule to get them done.

I understand that this is not what Ted and Cashmere originally signed up for,
and I would 100% understand if they decide to cancel their memberships. Sorry
guys, I have to protect myself.

I’ve updated the YouTube Memberships page with the new tiers, benefits, and how
everything works going forward:

[YouTube Memberships Info](https://linkarzu.com/posts/youtube/youtube-memberships/){:target="\_blank"}

I also want to thank all the other YouTube members that are part of the Discord
Server. We'll also keep having FurryConf every once in a while. Well… I renamed
it to `Open Terminal` because people think that we're actually furries and tend
to skip those videos.

[Past FurryConf episodes can be found here](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZWMav2s1MZSKO3uLD5-gB5tK_8lH_rAz){:target="\_blank"}
