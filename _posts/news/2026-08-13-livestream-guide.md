## Before the livestream

Please join around 15 minutes before the livestream starts. We will
use that time to quickly test the cameras and set audio levels.

Please use headphones during the livestream. This prevents the stream
audio from feeding back into your microphone.

## Joining the call

You will receive two links: one for your camera and microphone, and
one for screen sharing. You do not need to install any additional
tools. Open both links in your browser.

Use the camera-and-microphone link for all audio. Select your
microphone there.

The screen-sharing link does not use your microphone or speakers.

Google Chrome is preferred. If that is not available, use another
Chromium-based browser. Firefox is the last option and may not work as
well.

## Camera framing

Center yourself in the camera frame, with the top of your head close
to the top of the image. Use the reference image below as a guide.

<!-- prettier-ignore -->
![Image](./../../assets/img/imgs/260813-camera-directions.avif){: width="500" }

If you use a Mac and an iPhone, you can use Apple's Continuity Camera.
Your iPhone can be recognized automatically as a camera by your Mac.

## Audio

An external studio microphone is preferred, but it is not required.
Use any microphone you have available.

Laptop microphones are fine if you don't have a dedicated mic. But
audio quality will not be as good.
